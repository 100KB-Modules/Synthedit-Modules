FDelay is a modulated delay with feedback and lowpass filtering.

Description:
A flexible delay effect offering up to 10 seconds of delay time, audio-rate modulation, feedback, and a variable one-pole lowpass filter on the output.

Pins:
Input: Audio signal to delay.
Modulation: Controls delay time (0.5 = center, lower = longer delay).
Feedback: Feedback amount.
Cut: Lowpass filter cutoff frequency.
Output: Processed delayed signal.
Max Delay (sec): Maximum delay time (0.001–10s).

Usage:
Excellent for traditional echoes, dub delays, chorus, flanger, and experimental textures. Great for both subtle movement and extreme modulated effects.
Note: The delay buffer is cleared when changing Max Delay.
