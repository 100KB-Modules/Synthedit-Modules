100KB-24dB-Lowpass-Filter is a 24dB/octave low-pass filter with resonance and LP/BP morph.

Description:
Four-pole analog-style filter with soft clipping and 2x oversampling for warm, musical sound.

Pins:
Input – Audio input
Cutoff – Frequency (0.0 – 1.0)
Q – Resonance (0 – 50)
LpBp – LP/BP mix (0 = Lowpass, 1 = Bandpass)
Output – Filtered output

Usage:
Great for synths, drums, and subtractive sound design. Modulate Cutoff and LpBp for movement.
