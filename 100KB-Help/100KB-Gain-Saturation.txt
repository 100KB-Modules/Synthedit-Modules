100KB-Gain-Saturation is a smooth waveshaper / saturator.

Description:
Simple yet musical saturation with Drive and Saturation controls based on a cubic soft-clipping curve.

Pins:
Input – Audio input
Drive – Input gain / intensity
Sat – Saturation amount
Output – Saturated output

Usage:
Add warmth, grit, or gentle distortion to audio signals. Great on drums, synths, or vocals.
