100KB-5-BandPass-Filter is a multi-band resonant bandpass filter bank.
Description:
Up to five independent 2nd-order bandpass filters with selectable active band count and coefficient update rate.
Pins:

Input 1–5 – Audio inputs
Frequency 1–5 – Center frequencies
Resonance 1–5 – Bandwidth / Q
Output 1–5 – Filtered outputs
Active – Number of active bands (0–5)
Update – Coefficient update rate

Usage:
Great for multi-band processing, formant filtering, or parallel resonant effects.
